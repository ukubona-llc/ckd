Core Innovation

Grey-Aware Policy (GAP)

Standard PRISMA pipelines discard grey literature or relegate it to a supplement. GAP treats MoH reports, NPA briefs, and district surveillance data as first-class evidence alongside peer-reviewed sources.

The methodological contribution is not "we used AI for a systematic review." It is a named, reproducible pipeline that changes what counts as evidence — and therefore changes what a funder, planner, or clinician can act on.

If excluding grey data materially shifts the pooled estimate, you have just demonstrated why GAP matters. That discordance is the methodology paper.

High-throughput means the factory is the product. The CKD and HIV reviews are proof-of-concept runs. The engine scales to every major disease category in Uganda's 20-year NPA roadmap.

What We're Hunting

The Signal Space

🗺️
Geographic Clustering
CKD burden distributed independently of HIV prevalence maps — suggesting autonomous drivers (hypertension, diabetes, APOL1) invisible in journals but present in district grey data.
⏱️
Temporal Signal
CKD burden rising after ART success. The HIVAN story hiding in plain sight — long-term renal consequence of HIV survival, visible only across time-series in MoH surveillance.
📊
Grey–Published Divergence
Where MoH and NPA numbers tell a materially different story than journals. The GAP methodology's killer demonstration: pooled estimate shifts when grey data is included or excluded.
🧬
APOL1 × HIV Interaction
CKD in sub-Saharan Africa has a unique genetic-infectious profile. The composite run tests whether the CKD × HIV intersection is additive, synergistic, or clustered — not yet characterised for Uganda.
💊
TDF Nephrotoxicity Burden
Recent SSA meta-analyses (2025) show high pooled CKD prevalence in PLWHIV on TDF regimens. Uganda-specific disaggregation remains a gap. The HIV pure run resolves this.
🏥
Infrastructure Mismatch
Where CKD/ESRD burden is highest vs where dialysis and transplant infrastructure currently sits. NPA-05 heat map makes this visible. Composite run quantifies the gap for national planning.
